\chapter{Conflict}
\label{app:conflict}
We focused on consumption related conflict, filtering the dataset \textcite{acled2025} to include only the event not related to predation behaviour. Figure \ref{conflitti} analyse the temporal trend of conflicts from 2009 to 2019. I t shows that meanwhile rapacity conflicts present aa sharp increase, the trend of non rapacity once is increasing but with a slower speed. This suggest that the two categories are influenced, in part, by different factors.  
\begin{figure}[!htbp]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{con.pdf}
	\caption{Dominant conflict type}
	\label{conflitti}
\end{figure}

Non-Rapacity events average 0.66 per ADM2-year (SD 2.40), with zeros in 0.80 of observations. Peak activity occurs in 2020 (Total=336).
Rapacity events average 0.14 per ADM2-year (SD 0.61), with zeros in 0.91 of observations. Peak activity occurs in 2023 (Total=57).
